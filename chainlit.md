# Deep Research Agent 🚀

An autonomous multi-agent research assistant built with LangGraph and LangChain.

### Capabilities:
- **Autonomous Search**: Dynamically decides search queries and extracts verified content.
- **Credibility Scoring**: Evaluates source reliability and filters low-credibility data.
- **Synthesis & Quality Reports**: Formulates comprehensive, citation-backed reports.
